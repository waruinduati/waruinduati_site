When Brand Becomes Power

Chapter 1 of Tale of Craft, Volume 5: Brand design and development

Design isn’t just self-expression. It’s a form of stewardship.

In the beginning, brand feels like freedom. Colours, type, ideas, aesthetics. You get to shape how something appears in the world. But over time, you notice a shift. People stop asking what you like. They start asking what is appropriate. What is safe. What is aligned. You realise that every visual choice now carries weight beyond taste. It affects trust, legitimacy, and perception.

Brand stops being personal. It becomes social. Political. Strategic.

You’re no longer decorating. You’re holding meaning on behalf of others.
