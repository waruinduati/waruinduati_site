My Work and the Madness in Between

The introduction to Tale of Craft, Volume 5

This is not a collection of highlights. It’s a record of what it feels like to stay inside the work.

Most creative narratives focus on beginnings and breakthroughs. The first idea. The big moment. The visible success. But very little is said about the long stretch in between. The period where the work is no longer exciting, but still deeply demanding. Where you are competent enough to be trusted, but not established enough to be comfortable. Where responsibility grows faster than recognition.

This volume lives in that space.

The Long Middle is where most real designers exist. Not chasing inspiration, not celebrating mastery. Just showing up, making decisions, carrying weight, and learning how to remain present in complexity. It’s where craft becomes less about expression and more about judgment. Less about ideas, more about consequences.

The projects in this publication are not presented as trophies. They are presented as situations. Each one represents a moment where something needed to be clarified, reframed, stabilised, or built to last. The work is here not to impress, but to explain how I think when conditions are imperfect and outcomes are uncertain.

Between the cases are reflections. Not lessons, not advice, simply observations drawn from time spent designing inside real systems, with real constraints, for real people. They are the quiet notes you only start making once the noise fades and the patterns become visible.
