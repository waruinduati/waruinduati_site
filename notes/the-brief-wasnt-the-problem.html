The Brief Wasn’t the Problem

Chapter 2 of Tale of Craft, Volume 5: Presentations and profiles

The most powerful design move is not creating. It’s framing.

Meetings become strange at this stage of your career. Everyone wants “something strong”, “something clean”, “something world-class”. But nobody can define what success actually means. You start to see that your real job is not to execute ideas, but to slow things down enough for clarity to emerge.

You stop solving problems and start redesigning questions.

The better you get, the less you rush.
